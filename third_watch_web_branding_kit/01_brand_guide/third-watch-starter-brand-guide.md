# Third Watch Studios - Starter Brand Guide

## Brand essence

Third Watch Studios should feel like quiet vigilance: a solitary tower keeping watch after midnight, with a small amber human light inside a vast dark field.

## Logo idea

The core logo is the tower, the window, the circular aura, and the wordmark. The glow is a premium digital treatment, but the identity should still work as a flat mark.

## Logo hierarchy

1. Full lockup - website hero, game splash card, pitch deck cover.
2. Emblem - favicon, social avatar, Discord icon, watermark, in-game UI badge.
3. Flat/no-glow version - print tests, small UI, and clean layouts.
4. Cinematic glow version - website hero, trailer card, and atmospheric splash pages.

## Website direction

Use a dark, quiet splash page with the full logo centered, an amber divider, and short copy. Keep the page sparse. Let the logo and atmosphere do the work.

Suggested headline:

> Stories endure after midnight.

Suggested subcopy:

> Third Watch Studios builds atmospheric narrative games about rescue, unstable worlds, and the human light that remains when impossible systems begin to fail.

## Do

- Keep the background dark and restrained.
- Use amber only as a focal accent.
- Preserve generous negative space.
- Test the emblem at small sizes.
- Use the no-tagline lockup when space is limited.

## Avoid

- Bright lighthouse beams.
- Nautical tourist styling.
- Pirate, fantasy guild, or esports cues.
- Film projector or camera-lens symbolism.
- Busy texture behind the logo.
- Making the amber glow too magical or overpowering.
